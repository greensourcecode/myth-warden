Privacy policy

Myth Warden. Last changed November 10, 2026.

Myth Warden is a free game. You can play the campaign without an account; playing online needs one. This page says what the game keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it.

Playing offline

Your characters, your settings and the keys you chose are saved on your own device (in a browser, in that browser's storage for this site). Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Playing online

When you make an account and play online, the game's server keeps:

  • your email address, to log you in, to send you codes (to check the address is yours, or to set a new password) and nothing else: no newsletters, no advertising;
  • your password, stored only as a scrambled hash that can't be turned back into it;
  • your characters and their progress, your friends, your guild, your ratings and your place on the leaderboards (other players see your characters' names, not your email);
  • what you write in the chat: it goes to the players it's meant for, and the last few lines of each player are kept for a while so moderators can look into a report;
  • reports players make, and what moderators decide;
  • the internet address and a random device code your game plays from, so a banned player can't simply come back (an address is kept for up to 7 days after a ban);
  • error reports: when the game hits a programming error, it sends the error's text and the game's version, so it can be fixed;
  • the server's own logs of what it does.

Your device also keeps your login (not your password) so you don't have to type it each time, until you choose LOG OUT.

Who else sees it

Nobody buys or receives your information. The server runs on rented machines (Hetzner, in the United States). When emails are sent, they go through an email service (Resend), which sees the address and the code. The game has no advertising and no tracking or analytics services.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your account is kept until you delete it. You can delete it in the game (the online character list: ACCOUNT, then DELETE ACCOUNT): your account, characters, friends and the rest go at once. Copies in the server's backups are overwritten over time: hourly copies within 2 days, daily copies within 45 days; a copy from the start of each month is kept as an archive.

You can also change your email in the same place. To ask anything about your information, contact us.

Children

Online play isn't meant for children under 13. If you're under 13, play the campaign (it needs no account) and don't make an online account.
